Free D&D 5e tools

Calculators for the parts of 5e that need arithmetic. They run in your browser, need no account and cover both the 2014 and 2024 rules.

  • Point buy calculator

    Spend 27 points on your ability scores, add 2014 racial or 2024 background bonuses and share the build as a link.

  • Encounter calculator

    Add your party and monsters to see total XP, difficulty and XP per character under the 2024 budgets or the 2014 thresholds.

  • Dice roller

    Roll any dice notation, from 1d20+5 to 4d6kh3, with advantage, disadvantage and a history of your last rolls.

  • Ability score roller

    Roll 4d6 drop lowest six times, see every die and what the set is worth in point buy.

  • Multiclass spell slot calculator

    Combine full, half and third casters into one spellcaster level and slot count, with warlock Pact Magic kept separate.

Running the game?
